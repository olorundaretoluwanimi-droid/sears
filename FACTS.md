## Inferences and wording choices
- "the largest order for Sears houses anyone knows of" = Wikipedia's "largest known order".
- "a few years later" (1921 catalog) and "around 1920" for the undated street photos (houses built 1918–19, catalog 1921).
- "local historians say" for Schoper (single local-history source).
- The Carlin is shown as "one of the designs Sears was selling that year"; the film does not claim it was built in Carlinville.
- The rail map is a schematic drawn from Natural Earth (public domain) data, today's network, labelled "schematic".
